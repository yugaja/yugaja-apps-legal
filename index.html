Privacy Policy

Yugaja Internet Radio · Effective date: 18 August 2026

Yugaja Internet Radio is designed to provide internet radio playback, station discovery, favorites, listening history and stream recording features.

Personal data

Yugaja Internet Radio does not require an account and is not designed to collect personal information such as your name, email address, phone number or contacts. The app does not contain advertising or analytics SDKs.

Internet access and third-party services

The app connects to independent radio stations and online services to search for stations, retrieve station information, play audio streams and obtain available metadata and artwork. These services may receive standard network information such as your IP address, device request headers and the station or track search terms required to answer a request.

Depending on the feature and station, services may include radio stream providers, Radio Browser, UberStations, Apple iTunes Search, MusicBrainz, Cover Art Archive, Radio Paradise and station-specific public metadata services such as Naxi Radio. Their own privacy policies and terms may apply.

Some radio stations are available only over unencrypted HTTP connections. The app also supports HTTPS when provided by the station or service.

Local data

Favorites, folders, listening history, search suggestions, settings and recordings may be stored locally on your device. Recordings created with the REC function remain under your control and can be accessed through the Recordings section. The app does not upload these recordings to Yugaja Apps.

Permissions

The app uses network, foreground playback, wake-lock and related media permissions to play radio in the background. On supported Android versions, storage access may be requested when required to save, import, export or share recordings and station data. Bluetooth access supports playback through compatible audio devices. Permissions are not used to collect unrelated personal information.

Data retention and deletion

Locally stored app data remains on your device until you delete it using available app controls, clear the app's storage, delete individual recordings, or uninstall the app. Data processed independently by third-party radio stations or services is governed by their policies.

Children

The app is not designed specifically for children under 13.

Changes

This privacy policy may be updated when application functionality, third-party services or legal requirements change. The effective date above will be updated when material changes are published.
