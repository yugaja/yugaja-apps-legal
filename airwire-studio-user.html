Privacy Policy

AirWire Studio User · Effective date: 26 October 2026

AirWire Studio User is a control application for compatible AirWire audio devices. The application is designed to operate locally between the Android device and connected AirWire hardware.

Personal data

AirWire Studio User does not require a user account and is not designed to collect personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, contacts or precise location. The application does not contain advertising, analytics or crash-reporting SDKs.

Network access and data sharing

The application does not request Internet access and does not send application data, device identifiers, licence information, presets or camera images to Yugaja Apps or to third parties. Yugaja Apps does not sell or share user data collected through this application.

AirWire device and licence information

To establish a licensed connection, the application processes technical identifiers of the connected AirWire device, including its microcontroller identifier, together with a locally generated installation identity and signed licence data. This information is used only on the Android device to validate the licence and restrict preset files to the intended AirWire device. It is not uploaded to Yugaja Apps.

Camera and QR codes

Camera access is optional and is used only to scan AirWire activation and licence QR codes. QR images are processed locally on the device. Camera frames are not retained or uploaded. A licence QR image can alternatively be selected from device storage using the Android system file picker.

USB and connected-device access

USB and foreground connected-device functionality are used to communicate with compatible AirWire hardware, transfer control settings and display device status. Data exchanged over this connection remains between the Android device and the connected AirWire device.

Local files and settings

Application settings, signed licence certificates, installation identity, AirWire device identifiers and user-selected preset files may be stored locally on your device. Import and export locations are selected by you through Android's system file picker. The application does not upload these files.

Biometric authentication

If biometric or device-credential confirmation is offered by an application edition, authentication is performed by the Android operating system. The application does not receive or store biometric data.

Data retention and deletion

Locally stored application data remains on your device until you clear the application's storage or uninstall the application. Exported preset or QR files remain in the location selected by you and can be deleted using your device's file manager.

Children

The application is not designed specifically for children under 13.

Changes

This privacy policy may be updated when application functionality or legal requirements change. The effective date above will be updated when material changes are published.
